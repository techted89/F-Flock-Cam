F Flock Cam helps you stay clear of surveillance flock cameras.

F Flock Cam combines crowd-reported and provider-backed flock camera locations into a single map experience. Once a device is running the background action runner, the hybrid cloud backend (F-Flock-Cam server) watches the flock camera catalog from Deflock and your own reported camera locations, and nudges you away from cameras that may be monitoring activity in your mapped direction.

Features:
- Nearby flock camera discovery within a small radius.
- Route-based camera prediction along your mapped directions.
- Route adjustments that avoid known flock camera locations.
- Remote background action runner for start/stop monitoring sessions.

The app does not collect or sell personal data. Camera locations are used to keep users away from surveillance points.
